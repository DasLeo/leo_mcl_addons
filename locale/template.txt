Strawberry Seeds=
Grows on farmland=
Grows into a strawberry plant which in turn produces strawberries.=
Place the strawberry seeds on farmland (which can be created with a hoe) to plant a strawberry plant. Strawberry plants grow in sunlight and grow faster on hydrated farmland.=
Premature Strawberry Plant (Stage @1)=
Premature Strawberry Plant=
Premature strawberry plants grow on farmland under sunlight in 5 stages. On hydrated farmland, they grow faster. They can be harvested at any time but will only yield a profit when mature.=
Mature Strawberry Plant=
Mature strawberry plants are ready to be harvested. They always drop at least one strawberry, and occasionally drop two.=
